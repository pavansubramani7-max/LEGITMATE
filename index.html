Simple guide
Technical details and current limits

The project uses BM25, optional pretrained embeddings, rank fusion, entity extraction, evidence rules, a prototype knowledge graph, and optional AI drafting. OCR availability depends on local setup; POS and lemmatization remain teaching approximations, and no task-specific legal model is fine-tuned.

Account and storage protection

Accounts have individual salted password hashes and isolated document folders. Files are still plain local files without app-level encryption at rest; use operating-system account protection and drive encryption.

Private workspace per account
Keep control of each AI call. To stay in document-only mode, choose “Ask about documents” and leave the AI draft option off. For local file protection, use your computer’s account security and drive encryption.

Strict local-only switch: set AI_PROVIDER=off in .env and restart. The server then blocks all external AI calls, even if an AI option is selected in the page.

LEGITMATE helps review property and civil documents by extracting candidate facts, finding source passages, comparing possible conflicts, and organizing dates into a timeline.

Automated results can be incomplete or wrong. Check each finding against the original document. LEGITMATE is a research prototype and does not provide legal advice.
