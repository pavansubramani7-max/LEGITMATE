Privacy and data flow

Accounts and files: customers have separate account credentials and local document workspaces. Signing in does not send files to an AI service.

Document AI draft: only when selected, the question and matching text excerpts are sent to the configured Gemini or Groq provider.

Live AI chat: sends the chat message and recent turns to the configured provider. It does not attach document files or retrieved passages.

Protection limits: account passwords are salted and hashed, but stored documents are plain local files and are not encrypted by the app. Use this only on a trusted, OS-protected device.

Strict local-only: set AI_PROVIDER=off in .env to block external AI calls at the server, then restart the app.
